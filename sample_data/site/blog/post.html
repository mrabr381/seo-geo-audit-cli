How to pick a strong password in 60 seconds

Most passwords are weak because people choose words they can remember. Attackers know this, and they try the common words first. A strong password is long, random, and unique to one site. This guide was published on 5 October 2026 and takes about 60 seconds to read. It covers 3 steps and 2 common mistakes.

Why does length matter more than complexity?

Every extra character multiplies the guessing time. A 16-character random password would take centuries to crack with current hardware, while an 8-character one can fall in hours.

How do I remember a random password?

You do not. Use a password manager: it generates, stores and fills passwords for you. You only remember one master password.

What if a site is breached?

Because each password is unique, a breach at one site does not expose your other accounts. Change the one password and move on.

  2. Set the length to 16 or more
  3. Copy the result into your password manager
