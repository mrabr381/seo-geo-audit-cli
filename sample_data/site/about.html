About Widgetly

Our story

Widgetly started in 2024 as a weekend project. One person wanted a unit converter that did not ask for an email address, so they built one, then another, and the collection grew. Today the site has 12 tools and about 40,000 visitors a month.

What we believe

Simple tools should be free and private. No accounts, no tracking, no uploads. The site earns nothing and costs very little to run, because every tool is a static page.
