Free online tools, right in your browser

Widgetly is a small collection of free tools for everyday jobs. Convert units, generate passwords, count words and check colours — all without signing up. The site launched on 12 March 2024 and now serves over 40,000 visitors a month. Everything runs locally in your browser, which means your files stay private and the tools work even on a slow connection.

What can you do with Widgetly?

You can convert measurements, generate secure passwords, and check text statistics. Each tool is a single page with no ads in the middle of your work.

Why are the tools free?

The tools are free because they are simple static pages. There is no server cost per use, no database and no account system. The project is maintained by one person in their spare time, and the code is open source.

How do I use a tool?

Pick a tool from the list, paste your input, and copy the result. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Unit converter — 18 measurement types
  • Password generator — up to 64 characters
  • Word counter — words, characters and reading time
  • Colour picker — hex, rgb and hsl values
