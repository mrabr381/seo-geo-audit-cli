Every Widgetly tool in one place

Widgetly has 12 free tools. The unit converter handles 18 measurement types, from length and weight to temperature and data size, and it works offline once the page has loaded. The password generator creates random passwords up to 64 characters long, using letters, numbers and symbols, and it never sends anything to a server. The word counter reports words, characters, sentences and reading time as you type, which is handy for essays, posts and reports. The colour picker converts between hex, rgb and hsl values and copies the result with one click. The QR generator turns any link or text into a QR code you can download as an image. There is also a JSON formatter, a Base64 encoder, a lorem ipsum generator, a stopwatch, a timer, a coin flipper and a dice roller. Every tool is a single static page: no account, no upload, no tracking, and no ads in the middle of your work. The tools run entirely in your browser, so they are fast even on a slow connection and your input never leaves your device. New tools are added when someone asks for one — write to hello@example.com with ideas. The full list below is kept up to date, and each tool links from here so you can jump straight to it.
